— the laws of the substrate

The first invariants should be:

No silent overwrite. Every meaningful change creates lineage.
No unsupported elevation. An inference cannot become an observation merely because a model is confident.
No source erasure. Derived nodes retain paths to their source material.
No contradiction destruction. Disagreement remains visible until explicitly resolved.
No model sovereignty. Models propose operations; the kernel validates them.
No authority by similarity. Embeddings discover candidates but do not establish truth.
No scope leakage. Retrieval and graph traversal obey the same authorization boundaries.
Deterministic replay. Durable state can be reconstructed from accepted events.
Replaceable intelligence. Changing model providers cannot corrupt the shared meaning layer.
Harvest preserves tension. A current form may settle action without pretending every question is solved.

These invariants are the real intellectual property.
